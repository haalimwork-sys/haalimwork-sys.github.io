Troubleshooting

My computer doesn't appear in the list

Make sure this phone and the computer are on the same Wi-Fi, then tap Retry. Some office and campus networks block device discovery — if so, open the agent on your computer, read its IP address, and enter it manually in the app. Also check that the agent is running (look for its icon in the system tray) and that the Windows Firewall prompt was allowed.

The cursor feels laggy

Move closer to the router, or switch both devices to a 5 GHz network if you have one. Congested 2.4 GHz networks are the most common cause of lag.

It keeps disconnecting

Brief drops reconnect on their own. If it stays disconnected, check the Wi-Fi on both devices, then tap Retry. Your pairing is not lost — you will not need to pair again.
