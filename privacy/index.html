Privacy policy

This product works entirely on your local network.

  • No account is required or offered.
  • Your computer screen is never captured, viewed, or transmitted. The phone cannot see it — there is no feature that sends it anywhere.
  • No keystrokes, files, or browsing data from your computer are collected or transmitted.
  • No analytics and no advertising SDKs. Pairing keys stay on your two devices; input traffic between them is encrypted.

The full policy will be published here before public launch and will match what the apps actually do — if a future version changes any of the above, this page changes first.

Mousely Clone is a working title — the final product name will be announced before public launch. No cookies. No analytics. No accounts.
