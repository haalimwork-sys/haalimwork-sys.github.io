Everything your hand expects

A real trackpad

Smooth cursor movement with tap to click, two-finger tap for right-click, two-finger drag to scroll, and press-and-hold to drag windows and files.

🎤

A presenter in your pocket

Oversized Next and Previous buttons you can hit without looking, a laser pointer on the big screen, screen blanking, volume control, and Do Not Disturb while you present.

Private by design

Your phone and PC talk directly over your Wi-Fi, protected by encrypted, code-confirmed pairing. No account, no cloud relay, no analytics — your clicks never leave the room.

Use the trackpad at your desk, then flip to Presenter mode when you stand up to talk.
